Reseller FAQ

Can I set my own price?
Yes. Your floor is $297/month — that's our cost. Every dollar above that is yours, permanently, with no revenue share.
Do my clients know it's powered by Delegate and Done?
Only if you choose to disclose it. Your white-label is the face. We stay completely invisible.
What if a client wants a refund?
Refunds are between you and your client — you set your own refund policy. We guarantee our delivery quality; you manage your client relationships.
Can I use The Warm Sweep™ on my own list too?
Yes. Your reseller license includes one personal-use account at no extra charge.
What happens if I cancel my license?
Your license ends at the next billing cycle. You'll have 30 days to transition clients. We give you a 30-day heads-up before any service interruption.
